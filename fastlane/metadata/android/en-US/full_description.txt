AegisGuard is an autonomous, ultra-high-performance systemwide ad blocker, telemetry sinkhole, and privacy guard designed for modern Android.

Operating entirely on-device via a zero-copy local TUN packet filter, AegisGuard achieves the speed and efficiency of rooted kernel hosts modifications without requiring root access, without unlocking the bootloader, and without tripping Google Play Integrity or banking apps.

<b>Key Architectural Highlights:</b>

• <b>Sub-Millisecond Speed:</b> Implements RFC 5861 Stale-While-Revalidate caching. Repeated domain lookups resolve directly from RAM in less than 0.02 ms.
• <b>Dual-Stack Anycast Parallel Racing:</b> Queries Cloudflare (IPv4/IPv6), Google Public DNS (IPv4/IPv6), and active local network DNS resolvers simultaneously. The fastest verified wire packet wins in 1–8 ms.
• <b>Instant Local Rejection:</b> Returns RFC 1035 Null-Routing (0.0.0.0 for Type A, :: for Type AAAA) with a 1-hour authoritative TTL, forcing the Linux kernel to reject ad connections locally (ECONNREFUSED) in 0.0001 ms with zero network lag or connection timeout.
• <b>Autonomous 5-Hour Threat Feed Updates:</b> Keeps defense signatures continuously updated against modern dynamic ad networks, popups, and surveillance beacons with multi-mirror failover (StevenBlack, AdGuard, OISD).
• <b>Comprehensive Protection:</b> Blocks mobile gaming interstitials, video pre-rolls, cross-app attribution beacons (AppsFlyer, Adjust, Branch, Kochava), OEM firmware telemetry (Xiaomi, Samsung, Realme, Oppo, Vivo, Huawei, Transsion), and canvas/hardware fingerprinting probes.
• <b>100% Fail-Safe Whitelisting:</b> Mission-critical services (WhatsApp, Telegram MTProto, YouTube 4K, Twitch, Spotify, Instagram, Reddit, and Android system connectivity checks) are fast-pathed at Step 0 (< 0.001 ms), guaranteeing zero disruption to authentic apps.
• <b>Pure Native Architecture:</b> ~1.0 MB APK, 14 MB RAM footprint, zero third-party SDKs or tracking libraries, and 0.00% idle battery consumption.
• <b>Tesla Obsidian UI:</b> Minimalist aesthetic with a circular tactile actuator button, quick settings notification shade tile, and a pinned live status card showing real-time interception statistics.
